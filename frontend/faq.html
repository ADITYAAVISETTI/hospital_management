Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about appointments, reports, visiting and payments.

Appointments

How do I book an appointment?

Click Book Now, choose a doctor, pick a date and time, then log in or create a free account to confirm. You'll get an appointment number instantly, and an email confirmation if you gave us your email.

Can I book for my child or parent?

Yes. In your patient portal, open Family and add them. When you book, choose who the appointment is for. Each family member gets their own UHID and records.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Go to My appointments and choose Reschedule or Cancel. You can reschedule online up to two times per appointment. Please cancel if you can't come, so another patient can use the slot.

Why can't I select some dates?

Greyed-out dates are days the doctor doesn't consult, is on leave, or when the OPD is closed for a public holiday.

Do I need to pay online?

No. The consultation fee is paid at the registration desk when you arrive.

What should I bring?

A photo ID, your appointment slip or number, any previous reports and prescriptions, and a list of medicines you take.

Reports & records

Where can I see my prescription and reports?

After your consultation, your diagnosis and prescription appear under Medical records in the patient portal. Lab and scan reports uploaded by the hospital appear under Reports, where you can download them.

What is a UHID?

Your Unique Health ID links all your visits, reports and bills. You'll find it in the patient portal and on every appointment slip.

I registered at the hospital desk. How do I use the portal?

If the hospital has your email, go to Forgot password and enter it. We'll email you a link to set a password.

Health check-ups

How do I book a health check-up?

Choose a package on the Health Check-ups page and pick a preferred date. Our team will call to confirm the time. Most tests need 10–12 hours of fasting.

When will I get my check-up results?

Most results are ready the same day or the next working day, and will be uploaded to your portal.

Visiting, insurance & emergencies

What are the visiting hours?

Wards: 11:00 AM–1:00 PM and 5:00–7:00 PM. ICU: 5:00–6:00 PM, one visitor at a time. See Contact & visiting hours.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes, we offer cashless treatment with major insurers and TPAs. Please bring your insurance card and a photo ID, and contact the insurance desk before planned admissions.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call 108 for an ambulance or come straight to our 24x7 Emergency department. Do not use online booking for emergencies.
