Terms of Use

The rules for using our website, online booking and patient portal.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This website is not for emergencies. If you or someone else needs urgent medical help, call 108 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately.

1. Using the website

By using this website you agree to these terms. The health information on this site is general and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified doctor who has examined you.

2. Your account

  • Give accurate information and keep it up to date.
  • Keep your password secret. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Only add family members you are legally allowed to manage (for example, your children).
  • Tell us immediately if you think someone else has used your account.

3. Appointments

  • An online booking reserves a consultation slot. Please arrive 15 minutes early with a photo ID.
  • Consultation fees are payable at the hospital unless stated otherwise.
  • You can cancel online before the appointment time and reschedule online up to two times per appointment. For further changes, please call us.
  • Occasionally we may need to cancel or move an appointment (for example, if a doctor is unexpectedly unavailable). We will tell you by email where possible, and you can rebook at no extra charge.
  • Repeated missed appointments without cancelling may limit your ability to book online.

4. Health check-up packages

Package bookings are requests for a preferred date. Our team will confirm the timing. Prices and included tests may change; the price shown when you book is the price you pay.

5. Reports and records

Reports in the portal are copies for your convenience. The hospital's official records prevail in case of any difference. Please discuss results with your doctor before acting on them.

6. Feedback and reviews

Reviews must be honest and about your own experience. We may remove reviews that are abusive, contain personal information, or are unrelated to the care you received.

7. Acceptable use

Do not misuse the site: no attempts to access other people's information, disrupt the service, or submit false bookings.

8. Liability

We work hard to keep the site accurate and available, but we cannot guarantee it will always be error-free or uninterrupted. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for losses caused by using the website, as distinct from the medical care you receive from us.

9. Changes and contact

We may update these terms. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated terms. Questions? Contact us.
