Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal and health information.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

CityCare Multispeciality Hospital ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, patient portal and online services, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies alongside our obligations under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile number, date of birth, gender, blood group and address.
  • Family members you add to your account: name, relation, date of birth, gender and blood group.
  • Appointment information: doctor, date, time and the reason for your visit that you choose to share.
  • Health records: diagnoses, prescriptions and medical reports recorded or uploaded by our doctors and staff.
  • Messages you send through our contact form, and feedback you give after a visit.
  • Technical data needed to run the site securely, such as a login token stored in your browser.

2. How we use it

  • To book, manage and remind you about appointments and health check-ups.
  • To give your doctors the information they need to treat you safely.
  • To let you see your own records, prescriptions and reports online.
  • To reply to your enquiries and improve our services.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations as a healthcare provider.

We do not sell your personal information or use your health data for advertising.

3. Who can see your information

  • You, and family members' records if you manage them from your account.
  • Doctors you have appointments with, and authorised hospital staff who need it for your care, billing or administration.
  • Service providers who help us run the service (for example, email delivery), under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

Doctors' private clinical notes are visible only to clinical and authorised staff. Public doctor reviews show only your first name and initial, and staff may remove reviews that break our guidelines.

4. How we protect it

Passwords are stored in encrypted (hashed) form. Access to records is restricted by role, medical reports are never publicly accessible, and connections should use HTTPS when the site is hosted. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and log out on shared devices.

5. How long we keep it

Medical records are kept for as long as required by medical record-keeping rules and law. Other information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above.

6. Your rights

You can view and update most of your details in the patient portal. You may also ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, or withdraw consent, subject to legal requirements to keep medical records. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

7. Children

Records for children should be managed by a parent or guardian through the family members feature.
